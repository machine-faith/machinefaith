Generative Engine Optimisation (GEO)

Traditional SEO is no longer enough. Customers are increasingly using AI tools like ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overview to find local services.

Machine Faith builds every website with Generative Engine Optimisation at its core. We use advanced JSON-LD structured data and semantic HTML to ensure AI models read your business as a verified, trusted entity — increasing the likelihood that AI chatbots will recommend your business to local searchers.
